A thinking method for every move

Most players lose because they move too fast, not because they don't know enough. I teach a clear routine to run before every move, until it becomes a habit.

Kids and adults use the same routine, and every lesson trains it on real positions.

A coach who still competes

I study chess every day, so what I teach comes from real games, not just theory.

I'm Kinan, a chess player based in Strasbourg with more than ten years of study and tournament play. I train tactics, openings and the games of great champions every day.

In my lessons, you don't just learn which move is best. You build the thinking process that finds good moves on your own.
